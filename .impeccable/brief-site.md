# Landing site (Persuade)

Scope: public landing pages in `frontend/src/pages/Site.jsx` (Home, feature pages, safety page), plus intro animation and device frames. Audience: contest judges first (Monday), then ordinary Uzbek users. Action: open the app ("Ilovani ochish") / try the live phone. Constraints: site copy unchanged; live phone with real app stays; not toy-like or over-colourful; smooth on low-end PCs; glass/blur moderate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A money plan set like Samarkand tilework: every piece lands exactly in its place, Mizan = balance. Refuses the fintech split hero (copy left, phone right, three cards under) and the cream-serif-lime look the old site had.

OWN-WORLD: Deep cobalt glaze (#0E2A5C → #0A1F45) and chalk-white plaster (#F4F1EA) own whole regions; turquoise (#1FA6A0) is reserved only for the live or moving element; ochre-gold (#C9962B) only as thin girih linework. Glazed-tile panels = the only glass (subtle sheen + light blur, solid fallback). Eight-point girih star module rules alignment. Display type: a geometric-humanist sans with weight; no serif.

STORY: Visitor sees the star assemble into the Mizan mark, reads the hook, sees the real app working in the phone, follows the formula drawn line by line, trusts the honesty block, opens the app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full cobalt field. A large 3D girih star (CSS 3D, slow rotation, pointer tilt) sits off-centre right, the live phone set into its centre like a tile medallion. Hook headline left-bottom at large scale, primary action beside it. Nav as a thin plaster strip.

FORM: Koshin geometry (Registan majolica), candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:cf5b35653589ba0c. Raises: girih module grid (from brick instructions); turquoise only for the live element (automata); formula drawn step by step (zine); calculator strict step order (installer); intro as one numbered, timed sequence (neon sign).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
